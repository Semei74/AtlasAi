## What is an ADR?

An Architecture Decision Record is a short document capturing an important architectural decision, its context, and consequences.

## Template

```markdown
# ADR-NNN: Title

## Status

Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded

## Context

## Decision

## Consequences
```
